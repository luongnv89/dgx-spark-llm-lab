The mark is a step-up chart: three bars climbing left to right. The muted bars are the baseline and the candidates; the tall white bar is where your configuration lands after a run; the green cap crowns the result that matters — a winner you can actually install with bench apply.

The palette is deliberately terminal-native: near-black surfaces like the machines this runs on, one neon-green signal reserved for highlights — never fills — because in m-bench green always means measured, not decorative.

Inter carries the wordmark: geometric, dense at small sizes, at home in a README badge row or a CLI banner. The hyphen takes the accent color — the smallest possible reminder that the tool is about m-benchmarks vs their-benchmarks.
